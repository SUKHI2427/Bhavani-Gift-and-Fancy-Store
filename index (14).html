Our Store Heritage

More Than a Shop — A Destination for Every Family Celebration.

Located in the heart of Senappa Building on Kanakapura Main Road, Bhavani Gift & Fancy Store was founded on a heartfelt principle: every celebration deserves a special spark, and every gift should bring an unforgettable smile.

Over the years, we have grown from a modest neighborhood counter into a vibrant, multi-aisle superstore serving families from Vajarahalli, Ganigarapalya, Raghuvanahalli, and Talaghattapura. Whether you need wedding glass bangles matched to silk saree borders, birthday return gifts in wholesale quantities, or festive Varamahalakshmi pooja accessories, our shelves are always brimming with fresh choices.
