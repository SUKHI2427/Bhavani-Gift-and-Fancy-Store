The story of our shop

Bhavani Gift & Fancy Store began as a small neighbourhood shop with one idea: everyone deserves a little sparkle, and every occasion deserves a thoughtful gift. Over time, families from Vajarahalli and Ganigarapalya made it their first stop for festival bangles, birthday surprises and wedding return gifts.

We choose every piece ourselves, from colourful glass bangles to dainty jewellery, so what you take home looks special and is priced fairly.
